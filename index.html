LoQHayDetrás

Short animated explainers in Spanish about the hidden mechanisms of everyday things, on TikTok and Instagram: @loqhaydetras.

About this app

LoQHayDetrás is a personal, single-user tool that reads the statistics of the account owner's own TikTok videos (views, likes, comments and shares) through the TikTok Display API and saves them in a private spreadsheet. It does not post, edit or delete content and does not access other users' data. It is not offered to the public.
