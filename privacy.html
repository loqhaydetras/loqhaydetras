Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who we are

LoQHayDetrás is a personal, single-user tool run by the owner of the TikTok account @loqhaydetras. Contact: joel.leon06@gmail.com

2. Data we access

Only after the account owner logs in and grants permission, the app reads from TikTok:

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar) – scope user.info.basic.
  • For the owner's own public videos: title, publication date, view count, like count, comment count and share count – scope video.list.

3. How we use it

The data is used only to track the performance of the owner's own videos. It is stored in local files on the owner's computer (a spreadsheet and a private credentials file).

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or publish the data.
  • We do not collect data about other TikTok users, viewers or commenters.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are kept in a private file on the owner's computer and are never published or sent to third parties.

6. Retention and deletion

Data is kept as long as the owner wants to track the account. The owner can delete it at any time, and can revoke the app's access in TikTok (Security & permissions > Manage app permissions), after which no further data can be read.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is used only by its adult owner.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
