1. What this app is

LoQHayDetrás is a personal, single-user tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @loqhaydetras. It reads the statistics (views, likes, comments and shares) of that account's own public videos and stores them in a private spreadsheet. It is not offered to the public.

2. Who can use it

Only the account owner. The app is not intended for, and does not accept, other users.

3. What the app does and does not do

  • It only reads data through the TikTok Display API, after the account owner has granted access.
  • It does not post, edit, delete or comment on any content.
  • It does not contact other TikTok users and does not collect data about them.

4. Access and revocation

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the TikTok app settings (Security & permissions > Manage app permissions). Once access is revoked, the app can no longer read any data.

5. No warranty

The app is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind. Statistics shown depend on what TikTok returns and may be delayed or incomplete.
